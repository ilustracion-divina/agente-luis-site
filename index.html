Agente Luis

Agente Luis is a private personal assistant application designed to securely access authorized Google Drive files for document retrieval, organization, and analysis.

Access to Google Drive is authorized by the account owner through Google OAuth. Agente Luis only accesses files and information that the user has explicitly authorized the application to access.

Agente Luis is not offered as a public service and does not sell, rent, or share Google user data for advertising purposes.
